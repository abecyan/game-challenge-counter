What it does

After an authenticated player completes a VALORANT match, the planned integration will use completed-match statistics such as kills and ACEs to update a challenge counter displayed on the player's livestream.

Planned player flow after approval and RSO implementation

1. Open Game Challenge Counter.
2. Choose to link a Riot account using Riot Sign On (RSO).
3. Explicitly opt in to sharing VALORANT data with the application.
4. Complete a VALORANT match.
5. The application retrieves the authenticated player's completed-match data through supported Riot services.
6. Configured kill/ACE rules update the livestream challenge counter.
7. The counter is displayed in the player's livestream overlay.

Competitive integrity

This is a post-match livestream interaction tool. It does not provide real-time opponent scouting, tactical instructions, or live information intended to provide a competitive advantage during a match.

Planned player data and opt-in handling

VALORANT personal data is accessed only for a player who explicitly authenticates and opts in through Riot Sign On. Account linking means gameplay/statistical data needed for the service can be processed and displayed. Unlinked players' personal statistics are not made available through this product.

Prototype status

The local challenge counter, configurable game rules, livestream overlays, and post-match integration framework are implemented as a working prototype. Production VALORANT access and Riot Sign On will be connected after the required Riot approvals are granted.
