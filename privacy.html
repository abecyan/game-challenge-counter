Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Current prototype status

Riot Sign On and production VALORANT data access are not available in the current prototype. The account-linked data practices below describe the planned integration. The current local counter stores settings and counter history on the user computer. This website does not provide Riot login.

Planned information processing

After explicit Riot Sign On opt-in, the application may process the authenticated Riot account identifier and completed VALORANT match information needed for the counter, including match identifiers, kills, round events used to determine ACEs, and related match statistics.

Purpose

Data is used to identify the authenticated player's completed matches and update their livestream challenge counter.

Opt-in

VALORANT personal data is processed only after the player explicitly links their Riot account and authorizes the application through Riot Sign On.

Sharing

The application is not designed to sell personal information. Personal VALORANT statistics of players who have not opted in are not made available through the service.

Security

Riot passwords are not requested or stored. Authentication is performed through Riot Sign On. Production API credentials are kept server-side and are not embedded in distributed client code.

Retention and deletion

The application is designed to retain only data needed to operate the counter and prevent duplicate match processing. Account disconnection and deletion of account-linked data are planned for the integrated service and are not available in the current prototype.

Contact

For prototype questions and bug reports, contact the project operator through GitHub Issues. Issues are public. A private channel for account-related requests will be provided before account linking is released.
