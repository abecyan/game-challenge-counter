PLANNED INTEGRATION / NOT ACTIVE IN THIS DEMO

The intended player experience

  1. Open & configure

    The streamer chooses VALORANT and sets KILL and ACE counter rules.

  2. Link with Riot Sign On

    After approval and implementation, the streamer is redirected to Riot for OAuth authentication. The app does not ask for a Riot password.

  3. Explicitly opt in

    The streamer is informed that linked gameplay statistics will be processed and may be displayed to livestream viewers. They choose whether to participate.

  4. Complete a match

    The planned backend retrieves the consenting player's completed match list and details using VAL-MATCH-V1. No real-time tactical assistance is provided.

  5. Apply rules once

    Kills and round-level ACEs adjust the counter. Stored match identifiers prevent duplicate processing.

  6. Display & control sharing

    The counter appears on the livestream overlay. Account disconnection and account-linked data deletion are planned before public VALORANT integration release.

Implementation status

Local prototype: counter controls, configurable rules, saved settings, and browser-source overlays. This page demonstrates their intended counter behavior with synthetic events.

Not yet complete: production VALORANT access, RSO authentication, consent and account-management flows, and the production backend for public distribution. This demo is not proof of live API integration.
